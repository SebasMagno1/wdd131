Sajama Store - Site Plan

Modern clocks and stylish sunglasses in one place

Site Name

Sajama Store

This name was chosen because it combines the idea of time (clocks) with fashion (sunglasses). It represents a brand that is both functional and stylish.

Optional domain: sajamastorebolivia.com

Site Purpose

The purpose of this website is to showcase and sell modern clocks and fashionable sunglasses made with wood. The site will provide product listings, descriptions, and a clean shopping experience for users looking for both home decor and accessories.

Scenarios

  • What are the most popular sunglasses styles available right now?
  • Which clock designs would look best in a modern living room?
  • Where can I purchase these products online?

Color Schema

  • #111827 (Dark Black/Blue): Used for header, footer, and headings.
  • #f59e0b (Gold/Amber): Used for accents, buttons, and highlights.
  • #f5f5f5 (Light Gray): Used for background.

Typography

  • Montserrat: Used for headings (modern and bold look).
  • Open Sans: Used for body text (clean and readable).
